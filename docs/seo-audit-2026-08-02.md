# Classical SEO audit, 2026-08-02

**Everything here was measured against a real production build**, not against the
dev server and not against the source files. The build was made with
`NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://mazj.sa` and `IP_TRUST_PROXY=none` and served on a
private port, so it renders the **launch** configuration: robots.txt unblocked,
every canonical on a real domain. The pre-launch deploy cannot show any of this,
because it correctly serves `Disallow: /`.

---

## PLAIN

The technical foundation is in **better shape than most launched sites**. The
part that decides whether Google can crawl, index and correctly file a bilingual
site is not just present, it is exactly right, and that is the expensive half to
get wrong. Nothing here is an emergency.

What was actually broken fell into three buckets.

**One: the site was throwing away its own free advertising.** Twenty-two of the
twenty-six pages had no written search-result description, so Google was pulling
the first sentence of the page instead. Half of those came out either too long
and cut off mid-sentence, or so short they wasted the space. That text is the
only sales pitch a customer reads before deciding whether to click. **Fixed:
twenty new descriptions written, English and Arabic, all now the right length.**

**Two: the Arabic site was displaying its own opening hours backwards.** The
string said "9 to 5" and Arabic text direction rendered it on screen as **5-9**.
The same fault put the events hall's booking length on five pages as "5 to 2
hours". This is the exact misreading the 9-to-5 correction was made to prevent,
reintroduced by punctuation rather than by a wrong number. **Fixed and verified
with two independent Arabic text engines.**

**Three: the two halves of the site were not connected.** The language button was
a button, not a link, so as far as Google is concerned there was **no path at
all** between the 13 English pages and the 13 Arabic ones. Arabic is where MAZJ
has every ranking it owns. **Fixed: 52 real links now cross between them.**

**What is still open is mostly not code.** The biggest single risk is that
`mazj.sa` currently belongs to the Rekaz shop, and on the day it points here
instead, **26 Book buttons across the site stop working** and the shop's own 14
indexed pages die with them. That needs Rekaz to move the shop first. Second is
that the plan for carrying mazj.org's Google rankings across and the plan for
having both domains serve one site are **mutually exclusive as written**: only
one of them can run, and picking which is a decision, not a task.

## What is already right

Stated with the measurement, because an audit that only lists problems gives a
false picture of the codebase and invites a later pass to "fix" something correct.

- **hreflang and canonicals are flawless.** 26 of 26 pages carry a
  self-referencing hreflang, a reciprocal pair and an `x-default`; the canonical
  is a member of its own cluster on 26 of 26; the HTML annotations and the
  sitemap's 66 `<xhtml:link>` entries agree **key for key on all 22 URLs, zero
  mismatches**. This is the single easiest thing to get wrong on a bilingual site.
- **The hreflang HTTP header conflict is already gone.** `alternateLinks: false`
  in `i18n/routing.ts` is live: 0 `rel=alternate` `Link:` headers across 30
  captured responses. (Build-plan A1, done.)
- **robots.txt is 442 bytes of valid, correctly-scoped rules**, with every named
  AI-crawler group restating `Disallow: /admin` so group-selection semantics
  cannot leak the admin tool.
- **The sitemap lists exactly the right 22 URLs**: 26 pages minus the 4 noindex
  ones, all 200, all self-canonical, nothing indexable missing, `xmlns:xhtml`
  declared.
- **`/admin` is genuinely unreachable**: 307 to login, `noindex, nofollow,
  nocache`, `Disallow` in both robots groups, excluded from `proxy.ts`'s matcher,
  absent from the sitemap, and **0 anchors to it across all 26 pages**.
- **URL hygiene is clean.** `/EN` 307s to `/en`, `/en/spaces/` 308s to
  `/en/spaces`, `/en//spaces` 308s, `/index.html` 404s, `?utm_source=` still
  self-canonicalises, and a genuine 404 returns a real 404 rather than a soft 200.
- **No duplicate or over-length titles**: 26 unique `<title>`s, all under 60
  characters counted sans combining marks.
- **`aggregateRating`, `priceRange` and `Offer.price` are all absent**, as they
  must be. Do not add them.

### 🔴 One long-open question is now settled

`app/sitemap.ts` serves **22 URLs and zero events with `IP_TRUST_PROXY` set at
build time**. The previous audit could not tell "no event has a detail page" from
"the build degraded because the variable was missing" and said so. It is the
former: all 41 archive rows fail `hasDetailPage`, corroborated independently by
the rendered `/events` pages carrying **0** links to `/events/<slug>` in either
locale. The gate is correct and the sitemap is correct.

## Still open, in priority order

### 🔴 Launch blockers (nothing ships correctly until these are decided)

**L1. `mazj.sa` is the Rekaz shop today, and pointing it here breaks the whole
revenue path.** (CRAWL-1, LINK-1, LAUNCH-4, CANON-2, ADV-2; build-plan C1.)
26 shipped Book anchors across 12 of 26 routes resolve to 8 distinct
`mazj.sa` store URLs, and **all 8 return 404 against this app** (probed
sequentially). Eight redirect rules in `next.config.mjs` point at the same dead
URLs, and all 104 JSON-LD `Offer.url` emissions name a route that redirects
off-domain. `mazj.sa` also publishes its own live 14-URL sitemap, of which
**12 already 404 here**, and no redirect map exists for it.

🔴 **The recorded blast radius of "one constant" is wrong by a factor of ten.**
The store origin is hardcoded in **three** production files, not one:
`lib/links.ts:99` (`STORE_ORIGIN`, which emits all 26 anchors),
`server/rekaz/store.ts:53` (`REKAZ_STORE_ORIGIN`, the ticket button and its
JSON-LD offer) and `next.config.mjs:249-260` (eight literal
`https://mazj.sa/...` destinations). `next.config.mjs` has exactly one import and
cannot reach `lib/links.ts`, so changing the constant alone leaves eight rules
aimed at a dead host. Two test files also name it.

*Sequence:* ask Rekaz to move the storefront to its own domain (they accept
exactly one custom domain, so it is a hard cutover) **before** pointing `mazj.sa`
at Vercel. In the same commit: change both origin constants, and add 301 rules
for `/:locale/subscription/:slug`, `/:locale/reservation/:slug` and
`/:locale/merchandise/:slug` to the new store origin.

**L2. The 301 map and the two-domain plan cannot both run.** (LAUNCH-1, CRAWL-2;
build-plan B2.) All 25 rules in `docs/mazj-org-301-redirect-map.md` carry
`has: [{type: "host", value: "mazj.org"}]`. A Vercel domain-level redirect runs
at the edge **before** the Next application, so if mazj.org is marked as
redirecting to the primary domain, every request reaching `redirects()` carries
the primary host and **not one of the 25 host conditions can ever match**. With
the guards dead, 22 of the 25 legacy sources 404 and the three that resolve land
on the **English** page, not the Arabic one they came from.

*Decision required, and it changes the code:* mark both domains as **serving**
(no Vercel redirect between them) and keep the host-scoped rules, or set a
primary and rewrite all 25 rules without host conditions. Duplicate content is
already structurally closed either way, because `lib/site.ts` builds every
canonical from one origin with no per-host branch.

**L3. Nine of the 25 legacy URLs cannot match as written.** (LAUNCH-2.) They are
literal Arabic slugs, and Next's `redirects()` matcher sees the raw
percent-encoded path. Proven with an ASCII control: `/pricing` 308s via the
config rule, while `/%70ricing` falls through to next-intl's locale prefix, so
the matcher missed. Percent-encode every non-ASCII `source`, generated rather
than hand-typed.

**L4. Two more 301-map defects.** Every indexed legacy URL ends in a slash while
every mapped source is slashless, making each redirect a 2-hop chain (LAUNCH-3);
the rules match `mazj.org` exactly and will silently miss `www.mazj.org`
(LAUNCH-8); and two rows point at destinations that cannot receive equity, one of
them a `noindex` page (LAUNCH-7).

**L5. No Search Console property exists for either domain** (LAUNCH-12), and
whether the Change of Address tool even applies depends on the L2 decision.

## Refuted, so nobody re-files them

Twelve findings were killed by the adversaries. Two are worth recording here
because they look real and will be proposed again:

- **The Arabic archive date is NOT a bug.** `/ar/events` renders
  `١٧-١٨ فبراير ٢٠٢٣`, which does display with the numbers transposed. But ICU's
  own `Intl.DateTimeFormat.formatRange` produces byte-identical output for
  `ar-SA` (verified in Node), so this is **CLDR's canonical Arabic range format**,
  not a defect in this codebase. The proposed fix, `dir="ltr"`, was also measured
  and produces the identical string, because Arabic-Indic digits are class AN and
  stay RTL-ordered regardless of paragraph direction. Do not "fix" it. The
  hand-written `9-5` and `2-5` ranges were a genuine and different case, because
  they are not CLDR patterns, and those are fixed.
- **The "63-character `/en/faq` title" from the previous audit was a measurement
  artifact** (TITLE-5): 63 was the HTML-escaped length of `&amp;`. The real
  length is 59 and no title on the site exceeds 60.

Also re-verified as clean by an adversary who nearly filed the opposite:
`hreflang` is emitted by React as `hrefLang`, so a case-sensitive grep returns
**0** on a page carrying three. Any future "the site has no hreflang" finding is
almost certainly this.

## What nobody measured

Named so the gap is visible rather than implied.

1. **Real-world Core Web Vitals.** Everything was measured on localhost against a
   local build. Real TTFB from Saudi Arabia, real CDN behaviour and real device
   performance are all unknown and unknowable before launch.
2. **Whether Next applies a catch-all route's `generateMetadata` when the page
   body calls `notFound()`.** CRAWL-3's fix depends on it and it was not tested.
3. **Whether the 9 Arabic redirect rules match once percent-encoded.** Untestable
   until they are actually in `next.config.mjs` and rebuilt.
4. **www vs apex, HTTP-to-HTTPS upgrade, and which host Vercel serves as
   primary.** All Vercel project settings, none observable from a build.
5. **Whether anything external links to `mazj.sa/subscription/*` or
   `/reservation/*`.** Without backlink data, L1 is "our own 26 links break"; with
   it, it may also be "inbound equity is lost".
6. **The live Google rating and review count.** Still unresolved from the previous
   audit; needs a Saudi device. Do not quote a figure.
7. **Whether a native Arabic reader agrees with the new meta descriptions.** They
   follow the settled vocabulary in `TONE.md` and coin nothing, but no native
   review has happened.
